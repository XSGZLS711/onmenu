Install SoftPOS
    The shortcut opens SoftPOS as an app. Internet is required to sign in and sync. Prepare a trusted device to record sales offline.
    

    Change password
    Changing your web account password signs you out on all devices. Hold your current sale first if needed.
    

    Scan product barcode
    Point the camera at the product barcode.
    


    Prepare offline sales
    Save this shop's products and prices on this device. Offline access lasts 8 hours after the download. Walk-in cash, Card and M-PAiSA sales are available offline. Confirm external payments on the terminal or service before recording them. Stock is a last downloaded estimate, not reserved. Price, tax or access changes detected during sync need review.
    Pending sales stay on this device until synced. Keep the app open when reconnecting. Do not clear browser data or uninstall while sales are pending.
    

    Offline sales on this device
    Sign in as the original cashier to sync. Needs review means the saved sale was not accepted; keep its reference and contact your administrator.
